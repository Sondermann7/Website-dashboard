## What your child will experience

Kids ministry at Golgotha Fellowship is called **Feed My Lambs**. During the service, children take part in:

- Worship
- Learning from God’s Word
- Crafts and activities
- Prayer

## What we teach

We use curriculum from Answers in Genesis.

## Checking in

We use a child check-in process. If it is your first time, find a greeter when you arrive and they will help you check your children in.

## When we meet

Nursery and children’s ministry through sixth grade are available during our Sunday services. Children’s ministry is also available on Wednesday evenings.
